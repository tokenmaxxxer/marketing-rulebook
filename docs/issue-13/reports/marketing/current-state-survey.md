**2. vs.b 정규식 버그.** `marketing-messaging/hooks/messaging-gate.sh:219`: `cue = r'\b(vs\.|compared to|instead of|alternatives?)\b'`. The `\b` immediately after the literal `.` requires a word char on the far side; a following space (the normal punctuation form, `"vs. Acme"`) is non-word-to-non-word, so no boundary exists and the cue never matches. Verified: `'vs. Acme'` → no match, `'vs.Acme'` → match. `marketing-segment/hooks/segment-gate.sh:228` uses a different, unaffected pattern (`r'(^|\s)vs(\.|\s|$)'`) — defect is isolated to messaging-gate.sh. Existing test `tests/run-gate-tests.sh:81-88` (`msg-bare-vs`) passes today for the wrong reason: the cue is never detected as present at all, masking rather than catching the bug.

**3. bare 'unlike' 통과.** `marketing-messaging/hooks/messaging-gate.sh:225`: `has_any("unique attribute", "differentiat", "only we", "unlike")` — `has_any` (`:200-201`) is unanchored substring matching. Any occurrence of the substring `"unlike"` satisfies the check, including inside `"unlikely"` or in a sentence with zero real differentiation content. Specific to messaging-gate.sh's unique-attributes check; channel/segment gates have no equivalent gap.

**4. near() 앵커 vacuous.** Defined identically in `messaging-gate.sh:203-210` and `segment-gate.sh:199-206`. Used at `messaging-gate.sh:220` / `segment-gate.sh:229` with anchor `r'(^\s*[-*]\s)|([A-Z][a-zA-Z0-9]*(\s+[A-Z][a-zA-Z0-9]*)*)'`. The second alternative's repetition group is fully optional, so it reduces to "one capitalized word anywhere in the window" — satisfied by ordinary sentence-initial capitalization in virtually any English prose. Verified: `"Compared to nothing in particular, we are fine."` (cue present, no named alternative at all) passes `near()` as True, via the sentence's own leading capital. This defeats the intent documented at `messaging-gate.sh:214-217`.
